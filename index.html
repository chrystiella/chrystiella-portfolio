A little introduction

About me.

I’m a first-year Cybersecurity student at Stevens Institute of Technology, originally from Newburgh, New York. My interest in protecting technology started with CyberPatriot in high school, where I explored network defense and system security and spent three years as my team’s captain. Today, I’m continuing to grow my technical skills while finding ways to make a positive impact on campus. I also enjoy writing, meeting people with different perspectives, and staying curious about how the digital world works.
